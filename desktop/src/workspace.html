YOUR TERMINAL WORKSPACE

A place for every session.

Open a local terminal with +, or choose a session in your Outpost server, then Connect → Outpost Desktop. Local and remote sessions share the same tabs and splits.

Trusted servers

    Add your server address above to get started.

    Sessions that move with you.

    Drag tabs to reorder, out of the tab bar to make a window, or into another window's tab bar to merge. Press Esc to cancel a drag. Right-click a tab for move and split actions. Moving tabs keeps their connections and drafts. Click a tab to return to its terminal.

    Closing a tab always asks for confirmation. Closing a local tab ends its shell and running processes; remote sessions keep running. Unsent messages are discarded, and local terminal history is not saved.
