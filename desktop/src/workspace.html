      Sessions that move with you.

      Drag tabs to reorder or move them between windows. Drag a pane’s header to a terminal edge to split there, to the tab bar for its own tab, or outside all windows for a new window. The highlighted half shows where it will land. Press Esc to cancel. Moving panes keeps their connections, history and drafts; empty windows close automatically.

      The split button opens a new local shell beside the focused pane; right-click it to split top and bottom. To arrange existing remote sessions, drag their tabs or use ◫ in the session switcher. Drag dividers to resize; double-click a pane header to zoom or restore it.

      Closing a pane or tab always asks for confirmation. Closing a local tab ends its shell and running processes; remote sessions keep running. Unsent messages are discarded, and local terminal history is not saved.
